Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

What this page is

This page explains what information Asha's Ark collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We have tried to write it in plain language.

What we collect

  • When you write to us. Your name, your email address, and your message, when you use the contact form or email us.
  • When you give. Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email address, the amount, and whether the gift is one time or monthly. We do not see or store your card number.
  • When you visit. This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics. If that changes, we will name the tool here before using it.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, to process and receipt your gift, and to keep the records the law asks a charity to keep.

Who we share it with

Stripe, to process payments. Our form service, to deliver your message to our inbox. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to reply and follow up. We keep records of gifts for as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete what we hold about you. Email info@ashasark.org and we will respond. If you receive an email from us you no longer want, tell us and we will stop.

Children

This site is written for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Cookies and your device

We do not set cookies. The "Reduce motion" switch saves your choice on your own device, in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Asha's Ark, info@ashasark.org.
