Who we are

Asha's Ark raises money so that children in India whose families cannot afford school supplies, uniforms, or fees can go to school. You can reach us at info@ashasark.org.

Giving

  • Gifts are voluntary. Payments are processed by Stripe, under Stripe's own terms.
  • Each gift goes toward school supplies, backpacks, school fees, or warm clothing for children in India, as described in our donation section.
  • Refunds. If you gave by mistake, email us within 14 days and we will work with you.
  • Monthly gifts. You can cancel a monthly gift at any time, using the link in your Stripe receipt or by emailing us.

No promises about results

Asha's Ark does not promise a specific outcome for any individual child. We publish accurate information about how gifts are used on this site, and we do not publish figures we cannot support.
