I.

What we saw

My name is Niki. I started Asha's Ark with two friends, Arini and Sreekar, after trips to India to visit family. Those trips opened our eyes. We saw children who could not go to school because their families could not afford the basics: fees, a uniform, or supplies.

One thing stayed with us. A child's future was being decided by the price of a pencil or a textbook.

You already know what a pencil costs. For some families it is the cost of a future.

We realised how much we had taken for granted. A backpack, a notebook, clean clothes. For a family with very little, these small things can be the difference between a child in a classroom and a child kept home.

II.

Why “Asha”

We named the charity after my grandmother, Asha. In Hindi, asha means hope. Her kindness is the reason it exists, and it is the standard we hold ourselves to.

Our aim is simple: remove the cost barriers that keep children out of school. Supplies, uniforms, warm clothing for winter, and school fees.

Even helping one child would be worth it. That is what keeps us going.

IV.

How Asha's Ark works

Every gift to Asha's Ark goes toward school supplies, a backpack, or school fees for a child in India. We are run by three people across three cities, not a large organisation, and we keep it that way on purpose. We do not publish inflated totals or invented impact figures: we would rather tell you plainly what a gift does than make a claim we cannot back up.

Questions people ask

What is Asha's Ark?

A charity that raises money to cover school supplies, uniforms, fees, and warm clothing for children in India whose families cannot afford them.

Who will you help?

Children from low-income families in India who are out of school, or at risk of it, because of cost.

How will my donation be used?

Toward school supplies, backpacks, school fees, and warm clothing for a child in India.

Do you only cover school costs?

School is the focus. We also provide warm clothing, because a cold winter should not be a reason to miss class.
